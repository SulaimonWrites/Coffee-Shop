New to coffee? Start here.

Everyone’s first cup is different. These three ideas make it easy to enjoy.

Begin with milk

A latte is mostly steamed milk with a gentle coffee flavor. It’s the easiest way in.

Add a little sweetness

Honey, vanilla or chocolate softens any bitterness. Ask for it, nobody minds.

Ask us anything

Tell the barista what you usually drink. We’ll pick something close and make it your way.
